How the calculator works

Electricity rate

We use a domestic day rate of €0.38/kWh and a smart-meter night rate of €0.18/kWh (July 2026), cross-checked against SEAI price statistics and supplier standard rates. Pick “My own rate” to use the figure on your bill.

Heater size

Room size × a watts-per-m² rule of thumb: 45 W/m² for a well-insulated room, 70 for an average one, 100 for a draughty one. It is the size needed on the coldest days, not what you burn every hour.

Heater running cost

Size needed × hours × the share of that heat you need on an average day (60% by default) × days × rate. A heater big enough for the room reaches temperature and its thermostat cycles, so this cost is the same for every adequate heater. A heater that is too small runs at full power for every hour and the room never gets warm — we show that cost too, greyed out.

Dehumidifier running cost

Rated wattage × hours × days × rate, as a ceiling. Cost per litre = one day at full power ÷ rated litres per day. Wattages and extraction figures come from manufacturer specifications on each product page; nothing is estimated.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to run a 2kW heater in Ireland?

At a day rate of €0.38/kWh a 2kW heater costs €0.76 per hour at full power. With a thermostat it draws full power only part of the time, so the real cost is lower. On a night rate of €0.18/kWh the same hour costs €0.36.

Is an oil-filled radiator cheaper to run than a fan heater?

No. Every plug-in electric heater turns close to 100% of the electricity it uses into heat. If two heaters are both big enough for the room, keeping it at the same temperature for the same hours costs the same. Oil radiators feel gentler and keep radiating after they switch off; fan heaters warm the air faster. The difference is comfort and purchase price, not the bill.

What size heater do I need for my room?

A common installer rule of thumb is about 45 W per m² in a well-insulated room, 70 W/m² in an average one and 100 W/m² in a draughty one. A 12 m² room of average insulation needs roughly 840 W. A heater smaller than that runs at full power without ever reaching temperature.

How much does a dehumidifier cost to run in Ireland?

The 12 L/day dehumidifiers we compare draw 136–210 W, which is about €0.05–€0.08 per hour at €0.38/kWh. Running 8 hours a day for a 26-week winter that is at most €75–€116, and less once the humidistat cycles.

Which dehumidifier removes the most water per euro?

Of the models we compare, the MeacoDry Arete Two 12L Dehumidifier & Air Purifier has the lowest electricity cost per litre of rated extraction (€0.103 per litre at €0.38/kWh). Rated extraction is measured at warm, very humid test conditions, so real-world litres in an Irish winter are lower for every model — the ranking still holds.
